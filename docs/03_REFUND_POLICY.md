## 3. Refund & Cancellation Terms

### 3.1 Refund Eligibility
Refund eligibility is strictly determined based on the classification and delivery status of the digital offering:
1. **Recurring Subscriptions:** Optional periodic memberships (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annual plans) that provide access to premium functionality (such as enhanced multi-device cloud synchronization, custom business branding, or advanced analytical exports) are eligible for refund review if requested within the permitted refund request period following the initial transaction or billing renewal.
2. **One-Time Digital Purchases:** Fixed-fee, non-recurring digital purchases (such as lifetime software unlocks or permanent feature add-ons) are eligible for a refund review if the digital feature is technically defective, not delivered to the user's Account, or demonstrably fails to function as advertised.
3. **Digital Content & Virtual Goods:** Downloadable digital content, templates, reports, or virtual accounting modules are eligible for a refund only where the digital content has not been downloaded, accessed, or consumed, or where technical corruption prevents access and our technical support team cannot resolve the defect within a reasonable timeframe.
4. **Other Paid Services:** Any ancillary digital services offered by ENX Money will be evaluated in accordance with the specific terms disclosed at the time of purchase.

### 3.2 Refund Request Window
1. **Permitted Refund Period:** To be eligible for consideration, all refund requests must be formally submitted within **[REFUND REQUEST PERIOD]** (or, where purchased via Google Play In-App Billing, within the standard 48-hour automated window or within the 7-calendar-day developer review window) from the exact timestamp of purchase.
2. **Commencement:** The refund request window begins immediately upon the issuance of the digital receipt or order confirmation email by Google Play or the authorized payment processing channel.
3. **Strict Cut-off:** Except where mandatory local consumer protection statutes provide a non-waivable statutory right of withdrawal, refund requests submitted after the expiration of the permitted window will not be approved.

### 3.3 How to Request a Refund
Users seeking a refund must initiate the request through one of the following recognized procedures:

#### Method 1: In-App Refund & Support Channel
1. Launch the ENX Money application on your device.
2. Navigate to: **[IN-APP REFUND PATH]** (*Profile & Settings &rarr; Help & Support &rarr; Request Refund / Contact Billing*).
3. Alternatively, send an email directly to **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** (`support@enxmoney.com`) with the subject line: `Refund Request - [Your Registered Email]`.
4. Your request must include the following mandatory verification details:
   * Full legal name and registered account email address.
   * Google Play Order ID or transaction reference (e.g., `GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX`).
   * Date and time of the transaction.
   * Name of the subscription or digital feature purchased.
   * A clear, concise explanation of the reason for the refund request (e.g., technical failure, accidental duplicate charge).

#### Method 2: Google Play Direct Refund Channel
For purchases completed via Google Play In-App Billing within the last 48 hours, users should utilize the automated Google Play refund mechanism for the fastest processing:
1. Open a browser and visit: `https://play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory`.
2. Locate the ENX Money transaction.
3. Select **Request a refund** or **Report a problem**, choose the option that describes your situation, complete the form, and submit.

### 3.4 Refund Denial Conditions
To prevent abuse while upholding fair consumer rights, a refund request may be denied where legally permissible under the following circumstances:
1. **Late Submission:** The request was received after the expiration of the permitted **[REFUND REQUEST PERIOD]**.
2. **Significant Use or Consumption:** The user has substantially utilized, consumed, or derived primary commercial value from the paid digital service, invoice generator, or analytical modules during the active billing period prior to requesting a refund.
3. **Successful & Uninterrupted Delivery:** The digital product, feature, or subscription was successfully provisioned to the user's Account, operated without defect, and met all published functional specifications.
4. **Violation of Terms & Conditions:** The user's Account has been flagged, suspended, or terminated due to fraudulent activity, money laundering, fabrication of sham invoices, unauthorized scraping, or other material breaches of the [Terms & Conditions](file:///c:/Users/polam/Downloads/ENX_Money-krishna_dev/docs/02_TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md).
5. **Simultaneous Active Banking Dispute:** If the user has already lodged a formal dispute or chargeback with their credit card issuer or bank, internal refund processing is frozen until the financial institution concludes its investigation, preventing duplicate financial credits.
6. **No Absolute "No Refunds" Policy:** ENX Money does not maintain an arbitrary "no refunds" policy. Every timely, legitimate claim involving verified technical defects, unauthorized transactions, or accidental duplicate billing is reviewed in good faith. Mandatory statutory consumer rights are never compromised.

### 3.5 Subscription Cancellation
1. **Self-Service Cancellation:** Users may cancel an ongoing recurring subscription at any time without incurring cancellation fees or administrative penalties.
2. **Where Cancellation is Performed:**
   * **For Google Play Subscriptions:** Because subscriptions are billed through Google Play, cancellation must be performed directly through Google Play account management:
     * On your Android device: Open *Google Play Store &rarr; Profile Icon &rarr; Payments & Subscriptions &rarr; Subscriptions &rarr; ENX Money &rarr; Cancel Subscription*.
     * On web: Visit `https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions`.
3. **Halting Automatic Renewal:** Successful cancellation immediately stops all future automatic renewal charges at the end of the current billing cycle.
4. **Access Duration Post-Cancellation:** Cancellation does **not** immediately terminate paid access. You retain full access to all paid features through the conclusion of your current, paid billing cycle. At the end of the cycle, your Account reverts to the standard free tier.
5. **Cancellation Distinct from Refund:** **Cancellation and refund are separate processes.** Cancelling a subscription terminates future recurring renewals; it does not automatically issue a retroactive refund for fees already paid for the current billing cycle. A refund must be requested separately under Section 3.3.
6. **Uninstall Warning:** **Uninstalling or deleting the ENX Money application does NOT cancel your subscription.** Subscriptions must be explicitly cancelled through Google Play settings.

### 3.6 Refund Processing
1. **Processing Channel:** Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original payment method utilized during purchase (e.g., credit card, debit card, UPI, or Google Play balance). The Company does not issue cash refunds or paper checks.
2. **Processing Timelines:** Once a refund is approved by ENX Money, the funds are released through the payment gateway or platform provider. **Actual processing and reflection time in the user's account depends entirely on the payment provider, card-issuing bank, or Google Play**, and typically ranges from **3 to 10 business days**. ENX Money cannot guarantee an exact posting date due to inter-bank clearing cycles.
3. **Zero Deductions for Approved Claims:** For refunds approved due to technical non-delivery, system malfunction, or duplicate billing, the full transaction amount charged is credited back without arbitrary deductions, subject to any statutory platform processing rules.

### 3.7 Refund Disputes & Escalation
If you disagree with a refund determination, you may pursue our formal review and escalation process:
1. **Initial Review:** Reply directly to the refund determination email or contact **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** (`support@enxmoney.com`) detailing why you believe the initial decision was in error, including any additional diagnostic logs, screenshots, or transaction evidence.
2. **Escalation to Compliance Department:** If the dispute remains unresolved after support review, the matter is escalated to the Department of Legal & Regulatory Affairs at `[LEGAL CONTACT EMAIL]` (`legal@enxmoney.com`). An independent compliance officer will review the dispute within **5 business days**.
3. **Terms & Conditions Governance:** All unresolved disputes arising from or relating to refund determinations are subject to the informal negotiation and binding arbitration provisions outlined in Section 27 and Section 28 of the [Terms & Conditions](file:///c:/Users/polam/Downloads/ENX_Money-krishna_dev/docs/02_TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md).

### 3.8 Regional Differences & Mandatory Consumer Rights
1. **Jurisdictional Variation:** Refund and cancellation rights may vary depending on your country, state, or territory of residence.
2. **Preservation of Non-Waivable Rights:** Nothing in this Policy shall exclude, restrict, or modify any non-waivable statutory consumer protection rights guaranteed under applicable local law:
   * **Republic of India:** Consumers are entitled to all statutory remedies provided under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, for deficient digital services or misleading product claims.
   * **European Economic Area (EEA) & United Kingdom:** Under Directive 2011/83/EU, consumers ordinarily have a 14-day statutory right of withdrawal from distance contracts. Where digital content is supplied immediately upon the consumer's express prior consent and acknowledgment that withdrawal rights are waived upon commencement of access, this Policy applies accordingly.
   * **Other International Jurisdictions:** Consumers retain all mandatory warranties of merchantability and statutory refund entitlements mandated by local statutes.

## 4. Google Play Billing

This dedicated section governs the legal and operational relationship between ENX Money's refund practices and the Google Play Billing system.

1. **Merchant of Record:** Where subscriptions, in-app digital items, or premium modules are sold through the Android application, transactions are processed through **Google Play Billing**, where **Google LLC acts as the Merchant of Record**.
2. **Google Play Terms & Platform Rules:** All transactions processed via Google Play Billing are subject to the **Google Play Terms of Service** and **Google Play Payments Policy**. In the event of any conflict between this Policy and Google Play's mandatory billing terms, Google Play's terms shall govern with respect to billing and payment execution.
3. **Platform Refund Mechanisms:** Users may need to request refunds directly through the Google Play platform (via Google Play Order History or Google Play Customer Support) where applicable. Google Play maintains independent automated systems and criteria for evaluating refund requests submitted within 48 hours of purchase.
4. **No Ability to Override Google Rules:** ENX Money cannot alter, override, bypass, or reverse Google Play's automated refund determinations, payment risk evaluations, or financial settlement rules.
5. **Physical Goods & Real-World Services Exclusion:** Google Play In-App Billing rules apply strictly to digital content, virtual features, and software subscriptions consumed within the app. Purchases involving physical goods, real-world manual services, or offline commercial agreements fall outside Google Play Billing. As detailed below, ENX Money does not sell physical goods or real-world services.

## 5. Marketplace-Specific Considerations

1. **Non-Marketplace Declaration:** **ENX Money does NOT operate as an e-commerce marketplace, commercial exchange, or trading platform connecting independent third-party buyers and sellers.** 
2. **Inapplicability of Marketplace Provisions:** Because ENX Money is not a marketplace:
   * There are no independent third-party sellers on the platform.
   * There are no buyer-seller commercial relationships arbitrated by ENX Money.
   * There are no seller obligations, seller-funded refund pools, or marketplace sales commission deductions.
   * ENX Money does not manage multi-party commercial escrow or buyer-seller trade dispute procedures.
   * Customer and supplier ledger entries logged inside the Khata module represent private, offline records entered solely by the user; ENX Money does not facilitate, process, or refund debts recorded between merchants and their counterparties.
3. **Future Marketplace Roadmap Notice:** If the Company introduces multi-vendor marketplace functionality in future software versions, this Policy will be formally amended before deployment to explicitly define:
   * Legal allocation of refund responsibilities between ENX Money and individual third-party merchants.
   * Processing and reversal of marketplace commissions upon customer refunds.
   * Formal buyer-seller dispute escalation workflows and arbitration procedures.
   * Specific cancellation rules for multi-vendor commercial trades.
