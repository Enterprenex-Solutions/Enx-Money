1. Introduction and Scope

1.1 Who We Are: Enterprenex Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (“ENX Money”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the ENX Money mobile and web application (the “Platform”), a digital business finance and ledger management tool designed for Indian micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, ENX Money acts as a Data Fiduciary in respect of the personal data of its registered merchant users, and facilitates its merchant users (acting as independent data controllers of their own customer records) in maintaining their business ledgers.

1.2 Scope of This Policy: This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data collected through:

  • The ENX Money mobile application (Android, iOS) and web application.
  • Account registration, authentication, and biometric/passkey setup.
  • Use of Customer Management, Finance Mode, Analytics, Inventory, Transactions, Loan EMI, and GST Invoicing features.
  • Communications with our support and customer service channels.

This Policy does not apply to information that merchants independently collect from their own customers outside the Platform, or to third-party websites linked from within the app.

1.3 Important Note on Multi-Tenant Data: ENX Money operates on a multi-tenant ledger model. Merchants (the Owner, Admin, Staff, and Accountant roles defined in our platform) enter data about their own customers and suppliers (e.g., Khata ledger entries, invoices) into the Platform. In respect of this end-customer data, ENX Money acts as a Data Processor on behalf of the merchant, who remains responsible as the data fiduciary for ensuring they have a lawful basis to record that information. This Policy governs ENX Money's handling of merchant account data; merchants are separately responsible for their own privacy obligations toward the customers and suppliers they record in their ledgers.

2.2 Biometric and Authentication Data:
ENX Money does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any biometric data. Face ID, fingerprint, or other biometric verification used for passkey login (WebAuthn/FIDO2) is processed entirely within your device's secure hardware enclave (e.g., Apple Secure Enclave, Android StrongBox/Titan M). Only a cryptographic public key – which cannot be reverse-engineered into biometric information – is sent to and stored on our servers.

6. How We Protect Your Data

6.1 Technical Safeguards (10 Verified Security Controls):

  • Encryption in transit: All communication with our servers is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS 1.3 Encryption in Transit.
  • Encryption/hashing at rest: Passwords hashed via Cryptographic Password Hashing (bcrypt, 10 salt rounds); sensitive fields encrypted in the database.
  • Biometric cryptography: WebAuthn credentials use ES256/RS256 public-key cryptography; no shared secret is ever transmitted.
  • Access controls & Multi-Tenant User Isolation: Role-based access (Owner/Admin/Staff/Accountant) restricting which data each user type can view or modify; multi-tenant isolation enforced at the database query level (business_id and user_id scoping).
  • Server-Side Token Blacklisting: Logout immediately invalidates the session token via a high-performance server-side blacklist.
  • Input validation: Parameterised queries throughout to prevent SQL injection.
  • Logging redaction: Passwords, full JWTs, OTP codes, and card numbers are never written to logs in plaintext.
  • API Rate Limiting: Enforced across auth and transactional endpoints to thwart automated credential-stuffing.
  • Automated Database Backups: Point-in-time recovery archives encrypted and isolated in secure cloud storage.
  • Continuous Vulnerability Audits: Automated npm and Flutter dependency scanning to eliminate known security vulnerabilities.

6.2 Organisational Safeguards: Access to production data is limited to authorised engineering personnel on a strict need-to-know basis. Regular security reviews and dependency audits are conducted, alongside documented incident response procedures.

6.3 Data Breach Notification: In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users without undue delay, consistent with the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Rules, 2025, regardless of the perceived severity of the breach. Notification will include the nature of the breach, the data categories affected, and the steps we are taking in response.

7. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you (as a “Data Principal”) have the following statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access – obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities carried out.
  • Right to Correction – request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure – request deletion of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected, subject to Section 5.3.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent – withdraw previously given consent at any time, as easily as it was granted; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal – raise a complaint about our handling of your data (see Section 8).
  • Right to Nominate – nominate another individual to exercise your rights under the DPDP Act in the event of death or incapacity.

7.1 How to Exercise Your Rights: You can exercise these rights by:

  1. Using the in-app Privacy/Account settings screen or the online Account & Data Deletion Portal, or
  2. Emailing our Grievance Officer at privacy@enxmoney.com.

We will respond to verified requests within 30 days.
